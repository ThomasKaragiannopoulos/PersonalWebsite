What I built
  1. Achieved 92% user satisfaction during user acceptance testing, combining information from legal and geographic sources in Greek and English.
  2. Built a process that identifies the city block associated with an address, because Greek planning restrictions are often linked to that block.
  3. Connected each question to the correct block before searching the applicable planning legislation.
  4. Designed a seven-step process that checks and interprets the question, resolves the location and assembles a relevant response.
  5. Worked with data governance colleagues to ensure deleted personal information could not be recovered from retained backups.
