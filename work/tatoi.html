Tatoi Club

A personalised recommendation system that selects one relevant club service for each member every week and explains the suggestion in Greek or English.

How it works

1personalised suggestion per member, each week

  1. ~430k past interactions
  2. Understand member preferencesChoose a suitable service
  3. Balance familiar and new optionsSelect 1 of ~630 services
  4. Write a short explanationGreek and English; safety checks
  5. Learn from outcomesUse feedback to improve future suggestions
What I built
  1. Built a recommendation model using roughly 430,000 past interactions to select a weekly service from around 630 options.
  2. Added short, personalised explanations in Greek and English, with checks to keep the messages appropriate.
  3. Tested the approach with 50 simulated members over 10 weeks, recording a 94% selection rate in simulation, alongside comparative testing and 505 automated checks.
  4. Led the engineering work from early prototype to user acceptance testing.
